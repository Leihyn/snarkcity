How it works
  1. Bid in fog. A bid is a shielded Zcash payment. Its price is time-locked to a drand round, so nobody, the operator included, reads it before the close.
  2. One price. At the close the bids open and a uniform-price auction sets one price that every winner pays. Unspent ZEC is refunded.
  3. Claim on Solana. The tower rises with the share sold. Winners claim with their bid file, and the windows light up.
  4. Check it. Each launch publishes its viewing key, so anyone can re-run the auction from the chain and see every refund paid.

After you send

  1. Your wallet's sent list shows the payment. Shielded payments appear nowhere public, so your wallet is the only place to see it.
  3. A payment mined later is refunded in full to your refund address, never lost.
  4. After the close a receipt memo arrives at your refund address. Once the tower goes live, load this bid file in Claim: it says what you won, or that your ZEC came back.
